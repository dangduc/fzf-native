# fzf-native full benchmark follow-up — 2026-09-08T04:59:56Z

This report follows [the 2026-09-07 benchmark report](./2026-09-07.md).

It compares base `b21edb6` directly with final `15a8609`. Lower times are better.

## Outcome

Every symmetric fzf-native suite used fresh measurements from the same host.

Final won every process pair in these groups:

- all eight core scorer cases
- all seven full-corpus external cases
- all four Skim publication-latency totals
- all four Emacs fzf-native styles

The two geometric means across six micro-workloads improved by 1.020x to 1.028x. Several individual microcases remained near the measurement noise.

Two focused metrics became slower. The direct case-sensitive one-byte miss changed from 2.84 ns to 3.17 ns.

The persistent-session maximum changed from 2.389 ms to 2.709 ms. Final lost all five pairs for that metric.

The public one-byte all-miss path did not change at the reported precision.
